​​#WHEN GODS RUST

​It is a third-person souls-like game where the main ability is a special bar called the resolve bar. This resolve bar increases when players are able to successfully land a combo or parry an attack at the perfect time. Players can also craft and use different arms and cores; each core and arm has a specific stat as well as a specific load. Go above your load, and you get debuffs. However, keeping your resolve high can help you increase your load.

​#STORY

​You are an old, ancient robot whose main programming was to help humans; however, you were taken out a 1000 years ago. You suddenly wake up and find yourself in a world that not only treats robots above humans but humans are forced to worship robots as gods. Your undying will not to give in to these robots is your resolve, and since you are a low-spec bot, you can't use current-gen arms and cores, which is why there's a load limit.

​#CONTROLS

​WASD - Move

Space - Dodge

E - Interact

Tab - Inventory

Esc - Pause

Right Click - Parry

Left Click - Attack
