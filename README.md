​​**WHEN GODS RUST**

You are obsolete. You are still not going to kneel.

A third-person soulslike about an ancient service robot that refuses to worship the machines that replaced humanity. Parry, riposte, build Resolve, and carry more than your frame can handle. Land combos, parry perfectly, dodge attacks to gain resolve. And use it to block attacks and build resistance to POWERFUL gears.

​**STORY**

​You are an old, ancient robot whose main programming was to help humans; however, you were taken out a 1000 years ago. You suddenly wake up and find yourself in a world that not only treats robots above humans but humans are forced to worship robots as gods. Your undying will not to give in to these robots is your resolve, and since you are a low-spec bot, you can't use current-gen arms and cores, which is why there's a load limit.

**IN THIS DEMO**

* Level 1 - Basic level to teach combat
* BOSS (Chrono Croupier) - a two(currently) phase boss battle
  * Phase 1, King of Clubs
  * Phase 2, Queen of Spades
* Arms and cores to craft and equip, each with its own load and effect
* Parrying, riposte, stagger, and checkpoints

​**CONTROLS**

​WASD - Move

Space - Dodge

E - Interact

Tab - Inventory

Esc - Pause

Right Click - Parry

Left Click - Attack
